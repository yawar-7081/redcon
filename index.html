Our Expertise

Our Core Services

REDCON Engineering & Construction provides industrial fabrication, erection, equipment, scaffolding, and manpower solutions with a focus on quality, safety, precision, and reliable project execution.

🏗️
Structural Fabrication & Erection
Structural steel fabrication and erection services for industrial projects, covering fabrication, assembly, and on-site erection with precision and dependable execution.
🔩
Piping Fabrication & Erection
Industrial piping fabrication and erection services with careful attention to fabrication, fit-up, alignment, installation, and quality throughout project execution.
⚙️
Static & Rotary Equipment
Installation and erection support for static equipment, with focus on accurate positioning, assembly, alignment, and safe execution at industrial project sites.
🔧
Shutdown
Comprehensive shutdown and turnaround support for industrial facilities, including fabrication, erection, maintenance, equipment work, and skilled manpower to help ensure safe and efficient project execution.
🏗️
Scaffolding
Scaffolding solutions supporting industrial fabrication, erection, construction, maintenance, and other site activities with emphasis on safe and accessible work environments.
👷
Skilled & Unskilled Manpower Supply
Skilled and unskilled manpower supply to support industrial fabrication, erection, construction, maintenance, and site operations according to project requirements.
